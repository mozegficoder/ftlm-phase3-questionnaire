Tell us how your work is done today.

This short questionnaire helps us document the current ("as-is") state of your processes for Phase 3 of the study. There are no wrong answers — we simply want to understand how things work now, including where they are slow or still done on paper.

How to complete: answer for your own department or area of responsibility. Where you don't know, write "not sure" — that is useful too. Where a document exists, note it under "evidence / reference". You can fill this in on screen and use Print / Save PDF, or print it blank and complete by hand.
